Amrita Vishwavidyapeetham

            Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham is a multi-campus private deemed university in India. 
            It currently has 19 constituent schools
            It is spread across ten campuses in Coimbatore, Amritapuri, Kochi,
            Bengaluru, Amaravati, Chennai, Faridabad, Mysore, Nagercoil and Haridwar
