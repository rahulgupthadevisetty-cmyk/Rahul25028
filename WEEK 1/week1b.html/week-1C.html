ABOUT GAMES

Free Fire is a popular mobile game developed by Imangi Studios and released in 2016. It is an endless running game in which the player controls a character who steals a golden idol from a temple and is chased by dangerous creatures. The character runs automatically, and the player uses swipe controls to turn, jump, and slide to avoid obstacles like broken paths, fire traps, and tree roots while collecting coins
